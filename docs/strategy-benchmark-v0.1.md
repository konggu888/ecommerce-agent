# Strategy Benchmark v0.1

The benchmark runs three policies across repeated rounds and stress scenarios:

- NO_CHANGE: baseline policy
- FIXED_RULE: simple deterministic adjustments
- GAME_AGENT: constraint-aware adaptive policy

Scenarios include normal conditions, competition pressure, higher traffic cost, lower conversion, price war, low stock, tight cash, and demand surge.

The benchmark reports spend, revenue, conversions, ending inventory, ending cash, average ROAS proxy and action count. Results are descriptive simulation outputs only. A higher simulated return does not establish real-world superiority; promotion requires out-of-sample testing and safety checks.
